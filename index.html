    I'm a BS Computer Science student who ended up on both sides of the stack because the projects I cared about needed both. The work I return to has a common shape: a judgement a person has to make in the moment, quickly and with incomplete information — is this student coming in or going out, what is in the path ahead and how far away, is this banana still worth keeping. Software is good at the repetition and the measuring; the interesting part is deciding how much of the judgement to take over, and where to leave the person in the loop. I received the Best Emerging Markets Machine Learning Architect Award (International) for academic excellence in my third year at Biliran Province State University, and I've completed training in IT immersion at the University of Danang and Cisco's Networking Basics.
